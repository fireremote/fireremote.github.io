Fire Remote

Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Fire Remote is an iPhone app that controls your TV over your home Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Fire Remote has no user accounts, shows no ads, doesn’t use analytics or tracking, and we don’t run servers that receive your data.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Your TVs. The names and network addresses of TVs you connect to are saved on your iPhone so the app can reconnect.
  • Pairing keys. Keys and certificates created when you pair with a TV are stored in your iPhone’s Keychain.
  • Settings. Choices such as your accent color are saved on your iPhone.

Deleting the app removes this information, except Keychain items, which iOS may keep until you erase your iPhone.

Local network

Fire Remote asks for Local Network access to find TVs on your Wi-Fi and to send remote commands to them. Commands and the text you type with the TV keyboard go directly from your iPhone to your TV. They aren’t sent to us and aren’t stored by the app. App names and icons are read from your TV only to show them in the app.

Purchases

Fire Remote Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we receive no payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple to unlock Pro features.

Children

Fire Remote isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
